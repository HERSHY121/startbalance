## Email / form message

**Subject:** StartBalance — request to explore read-only Healthy Start card balance access

Hello,

We’re the team behind **StartBalance**, a small digital companion for people on the NHS Healthy Start prepaid card.

Today, cardholders who want to know what’s left before they shop must call the automated line (0118 338 5810) or check at a cashpoint. That works, but it’s a poor fit for busy parents and is especially difficult for people who are Deaf or hard of hearing.

StartBalance is in early trial as a **local tracker**: after someone checks their balance by phone or cashpoint, they enter that amount once, log spends, and see what’s left. Families find that useful, but the experience people actually want is a **live, trusted balance** in an app — the same number the issuer already holds.

We’re writing to ask whether **NHS Business Services Authority** (with **allpay**, the card issuer) would be open to exploring a **sanctioned, read-only** digital channel for verified cardholders, limited to:

- current balance  
- recent transactions  
- next top-up / payment schedule information (if available)

We are **not** asking to initiate payments, handle PINs, or present StartBalance as an official NHS app. We want a proper partnership or permitted integration so families aren’t stuck on the phone.

We’re happy to share:

- the current trial app and how it works  
- a short security and privacy outline  
- notes on accessibility (improving on the voice-only balance line)

Could you route this to the right Healthy Start / digital / commercial contact, or let us know the correct process for a proposal of this kind?

Thank you,  
**StartBalance project**  
[contact email / reply address to add]  
https://startbalance.github.io/startbalance/
